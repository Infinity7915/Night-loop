> **Still an early test build.** Please report anything odd in [Issues](https://github.com/Infinity7915/Night-loop/issues).

If you already have Night-loop, you don't need to download anything: open it and it updates itself.

## New

- **Seasonal themes** (Settings › Appearance, off until you turn it on). Around holidays, the Night-loop icon in the window, the taskbar and the tray dresses up for the season:
  - **Christmas week (Dec 18–30):** the moon at the North Pole, with northern lights, snow, pine trees, an igloo, a candy-striped pole and a Santa hat
  - **New Year (Dec 31–Jan 2):** fireworks, confetti, a lit-up skyline and a party hat
  - **Valentine's (Feb 8–14):** hearts, Cupid's arrow and a heart crown
  - **St. Patrick's (Mar 11–17):** a rainbow, a pot of gold, shamrocks and a green top hat
  - **Easter week:** bunny ears, painted eggs, daisies and butterflies
  - **4th of July (Jun 28–Jul 5):** red, white and blue fireworks, sparklers, a flag and an Uncle Sam hat
  - **Halloween (Oct 24–31):** a witch hat, bats, a haunted house, a ghost and a jack-o'-lantern
  - **Thanksgiving week:** falling leaves, pumpkins, corn, a pie and a pilgrim hat
- The icon on your desktop shortcut stays the same. Windows doesn't let apps change it.
