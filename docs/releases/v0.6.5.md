> **Still an early test build.** Please report anything odd in [Issues](https://github.com/Infinity7915/Night-loop/issues).

If you already have Night-loop, you don't need to download anything: open it and it updates itself.

## New

- **Pick on the live screen** (Settings › Macros, off by default). When you pick a click spot, a color, an area or
  a picture on screen, the screen keeps moving instead of freezing into a snapshot. A crosshair and the position
  follow the mouse, the area you drag is outlined, and the color under the mouse is read live. Pictures are taken
  the moment you let go, and the outline and labels never end up in them. Clicks still never reach the game.
  Turn it off to go back to the frozen snapshot.
- **Record, Play and Add block stay in sight.** With a long macro, the bars at the top of the loaded macro (name,
  Record, Play, Add block, the ticked blocks row) now stay pinned to the top of the page as you scroll, like the
  block editor does. They stop pinning while an options panel or block list is open and too tall to leave room.
  Settings › Macros › Block editor follows scrolling turns both off.

## Download

- **NightLoop-win-Setup.exe**: installs Night-loop for your Windows user and keeps it up to date.
- **NightLoop-win-Portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `NightLoop.exe`.

Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"** because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info →
Run anyway**.
