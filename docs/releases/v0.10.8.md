## Night-loop 0.10.8

- **Brightness range % instead of the Dimmer or brighter switch.** Picture blocks (Wait for picture, Find & click, Scan & turn, Image visible conditions) now have a number for how much dimmer or brighter than the captured picture a match may look. Blank is 6% (as captured, with room for screen noise), 0 is exactly as captured, 30 lets a copy 30% dimmer or brighter match. Blocks that had "Also match" turned on read as 50%.
