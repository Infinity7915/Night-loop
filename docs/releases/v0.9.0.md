> **Still an early test build.** Please report anything odd in [Issues](https://github.com/Infinity7915/Night-loop/issues).

If you already have Night-loop, you don't need to download anything: open it and it updates itself.

## New

- **Limits in Settings.** A new **Limits** box in Settings shows:
  - **Macros**: how many your account holds out of 15 (drafts included).
  - **New macros today**: out of 30 a day.
  - **Blocks**: blocks in all your macros together, out of 2,000.
  - **Blocks placed today**: new blocks you've added, pasted, duplicated or recorded today, out of 200.
  - When the daily counts reset: midnight UTC, shown in your own time with how long is left.
- **A heads-up before you run out.** Once 10 or fewer blocks or macros are left, a message shows at the top of
  the Night-loop window saying how many are left (inside the app, not a Windows notification). It shows again each
  time the number drops.
- When nothing is left, Add block, Paste, Duplicate and Record say so instead of adding blocks, and recording stops
  at the blocks you have left.

## Changed

- **No more 500-block cap per macro.** A macro can hold as many blocks as fit in its 200 KB size limit, up to the
  account's 2,000 blocks.
- Moving, editing and deleting blocks never count as placing. A new macro's starter blocks do count.

## Download

- **NightLoop-win-Setup.exe**: installs Night-loop for your Windows user and keeps it up to date.
- **NightLoop-win-Portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `NightLoop.exe`.

Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"** because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info →
Run anyway**.
