> **Still an early test build.** Please report anything odd in [Issues](https://github.com/Infinity7915/Night-loop/issues).

If you already have Night-loop, you don't need to download anything: open it and it updates itself.

## Fixed

- **Scan & turn kept turning the camera after it found its target.** As soon as the target is seen, turning
  stops. Night-loop then looks once more after the view settles and clicks where the target is now. After that
  it goes on to the next block. In games where the camera follows the mouse, moving the mouse to the target used
  to swing the view away from it. Night-loop now turns the target into the middle of the screen and clicks
  without moving the mouse.

## New

- **Scan & turn › Turn with**: choose Mouse (right-drag), Mouse (camera follows mouse) or Arrow keys.
  New blocks pick right-drag for Roblox and mouse for other games.
- **Clicks (1–3)** on Scan & turn and Find & click, like the other click blocks. Scan & turn also gets a
  **Button** choice.

## Download

- **NightLoop-win-Setup.exe**: installs Night-loop for your Windows user and keeps it up to date.
- **NightLoop-win-Portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `NightLoop.exe`.

Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"** because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info →
Run anyway**.
