## Night-loop 0.10.12

- **Big pictures are found at 100% again.** A picked area wider or taller than 200 px is shrunk to fit, and its pixels only lined up with the screen's by chance. Text and fine detail then scored as low as 86% on the very screen they were picked from, below the 90% Confidence, so Wait for picture kept waiting and never tapped. The screen is now lined up with the picture around each find, and the same thing scores 100%. Pictures picked from now on also remember their exact size.
- **100% Confidence works.** Scores are compared in whole percents, the way the test shows them, so 100% means "looks the same" instead of every last pixel identical.
- **Match colors on dark pictures.** Light text on a dark background could look a few percent dimmer when the box was a pixel off, so Match colors refused it on the same screen. Colors are now checked on the lined-up box.
- **Short, wide pictures (tabs, labels) are found about 6x faster** with automatic size.
- **On PC you can pick an area bigger than 200 px.** It is shrunk to fit, like on phones; before, picking one showed an error.
