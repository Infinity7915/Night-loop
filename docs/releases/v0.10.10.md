## Night-loop 0.10.10

- **Big pictures picked on a phone are looked for at their real size.** Android shrinks a picked area wider than 200 px before saving it, so with Size tolerance 0 the picture could never match at full size and a poor look-alike (even a plain screen) could win. Pictures now remember how much they were shrunk, and the screen is shrunk the same way before looking. Pick the picture again once after updating so it carries this note.
