## Night-loop 0.10.13

- **A grayed-out copy is no longer taken for the colored picture.** A grayed-out button or card has the same shape as the colored one, and it can be just as bright, so Match colors let it through: on pictures with many or pale colors even at 100% Confidence, and with Average colors on for most pictures. Find & click and Wait for picture then clicked it anyway. Match colors now also checks how much of the picture's color is still there. A copy that has lost more than about a third of it doesn't count as found (more than about half with Average colors or a wide Brightness range, since game lighting washes colors out a little).
- **It works the other way round too.** A picture of the gray one isn't found on the colored one. With Average colors on that only holds for strongly colored things, as before, because game lighting can tint a gray thing.
- **Test on screen says so.** When only a grayed-out or different-colored copy is on screen, the test says it found the shape but not the picture's colors.
- **What stays the same.** A picture with hardly any color (white text, a gray icon) is matched as before, since there is no color to lose. With Match colors off only the shape counts, so a grayed-out copy still matches there.
- **See-through buttons.** If the picture is mostly the game showing through a see-through button, its colors are the game's. With Average colors on, such a picture picked over something colorful is no longer found over something gray. Turn Match colors off for those.
