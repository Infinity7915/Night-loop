> **Still an early test build.** Please report anything odd in [Issues](https://github.com/Infinity7915/Night-loop/issues).

If you already have Night-loop, you don't need to download anything: open it and it updates itself.

## New

- **Match colors** on every picture block (Wait for image, Image gone, Find & click and Scan & turn looking for an
  image, and If / Repeat until with "Image visible"). On by default: the picture's colors have to match as well as
  its shape, so a same-shaped thing in another color is never clicked. Small lighting changes in the game are
  allowed. Pictures captured before 0.6.2 only saved their shape, so **capture them again** to use it (the block
  editor says when a picture is shape-only).

## Changed in 0.6.1

- **Picture size is automatic.** Every picture block finds the picture at any size, from 30% to 160% of how you
  captured it, so a target far away in a game is still found. **Size tolerance %** shows **Auto**; type a number
  only to limit it (0 is the exact size only).

## Fixed

- **The camera tilted up and down when centering on a target.** Scan & turn (When found: Center camera or Click it)
  and Center on target also turned the camera up or down to put the target in the middle of the screen. A target on
  the ground is always below the middle, so the view pointed at the floor. They now turn sideways only.
- **Clicking a target in games where the camera follows the mouse** tilts just enough to aim, clicks, and tilts
  back by the same amount, so the view ends up level.
- **Centering overshot on high mouse sensitivity.** The first centering turn is now a small one that measures the
  game's sensitivity, and readings thrown off by a moving target are ignored.

## Download

- **NightLoop-win-Setup.exe**: installs Night-loop for your Windows user and keeps it up to date.
- **NightLoop-win-Portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `NightLoop.exe`.

Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"** because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info →
Run anyway**.
