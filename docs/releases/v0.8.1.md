> **Still an early test build.** Please report anything odd in [Issues](https://github.com/Infinity7915/Night-loop/issues).

If you already have Night-loop, you don't need to download anything: open it and it updates itself.

## New

- **Choose where a picture gets clicked.** Find & click and Scan & turn looking for an **Image** now have a
  **click spot**. Once the picture is found on screen, the click lands on that spot instead of the middle, so you can
  hit a button's corner, the icon on a card, or the edge of something.
  - **Set click spot in picture** opens the captured picture big: click (or drag) the spot, arrow keys nudge it
    (Shift for bigger steps), **Middle** puts it back, **Save spot** keeps it.
  - The picture preview in the block editor shows the spot as a dot. Clicking the preview moves it too.
  - **Click spot across %** and **Click spot down %** show the same spot as numbers (0 = left / top edge,
    100 = right / bottom edge). Blank is the middle.
  - The spot stays on the same part of the picture when it is found smaller or bigger (Size tolerance).
  - Scan & turn's **Center camera on it** and the mouse-move camera click aim at the spot too, and **Test on screen
    now** reports where the click would land.
- Every block made before 0.8.1 keeps clicking the middle, exactly as before.

## Download

- **NightLoop-win-Setup.exe**: installs Night-loop for your Windows user and keeps it up to date.
- **NightLoop-win-Portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `NightLoop.exe`.

Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"** because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info →
Run anyway**.
