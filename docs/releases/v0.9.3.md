> **Still an early test build.** Please report anything odd in [Issues](https://github.com/Infinity7915/Night-loop/issues).

If you already have Night-loop, you don't need to download anything: open it and it updates itself.

## Fixed

- **Scan & turn and Find & click no longer click before the target is really there.** Before, one look that
  seemed to find the picture or text was enough to click, even if the next look didn't see it. A frame caught
  while the camera was still easing to a stop, a look-alike for a moment, or text read wrong once could set off
  a click on the wrong spot. Now they look again after a short pause and only click when it is still there in
  the same place. While the view is still sliding, they wait for it to stop and click where the target ends up.
- **Scan & turn (camera follows the mouse) no longer clicks blind.** If it lost sight of the target while
  turning it into the middle, it used to click anyway. Now it goes back to scanning.
- **Fewer picture look-alikes.** A faint or washed-out patch with roughly the right outline no longer counts
  as a match (normal lighting changes still do), and automatic size no longer shrinks a picture to a few
  pixels, where it looks like almost anything.

## Download

- **NightLoop-win-Setup.exe**: installs Night-loop for your Windows user and keeps it up to date.
- **NightLoop-win-Portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `NightLoop.exe`.

Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"** because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info →
Run anyway**.
