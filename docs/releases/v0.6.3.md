> **Still an early test build.** Please report anything odd in [Issues](https://github.com/Infinity7915/Night-loop/issues).

If you already have Night-loop, you don't need to download anything: open it and it updates itself.

## Changed

- **Centering looks at the target in two steps.** Scan & turn (When found: Center camera on it) and Center on target
  first turn sideways until the target is in the middle left to right. Only then do they look up or down until it is
  in the middle top to bottom. A single turn never goes sideways and up or down at the same time, so the camera
  can't swing off at an angle.
- Up and down use their own sensitivity reading, starting from the sideways one, so the tilt settles in a few small
  steps instead of overshooting.
- Scan & turn's **Click it** in games where the camera follows the mouse is unchanged: it centers sideways, tilts just
  enough to aim, clicks and tilts back level.

## Download

- **NightLoop-win-Setup.exe**: installs Night-loop for your Windows user and keeps it up to date.
- **NightLoop-win-Portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `NightLoop.exe`.

Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"** because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info →
Run anyway**.
