## Night-loop 0.10.6

- **Dimmer or brighter.** Picture blocks (and If / Repeat until with Image visible) have a new switch under Match colors. **Exact colors** (the default) only finds the picture in the colors you captured, so a dimmer or brighter copy isn't clicked. **Also match** averages the screen's colors to the picture's brightness first, so a dimmer or brighter copy matches too, like before this version.
- **Set click spot on screen.** Find & click and Scan & turn have a new button next to "Set click spot in picture". Show the picture on screen, press it, and click anywhere: inside the picture or beside it. The spot is saved relative to the picture, so it follows the picture wherever it is found (and grows or shrinks with it). The % fields now also take numbers below 0 and above 100 for spots beside the picture.
