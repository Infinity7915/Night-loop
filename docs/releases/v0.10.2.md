> **Still an early test build.** Please report anything odd in [Issues](https://github.com/Infinity7915/Night-loop/issues).

If you already have Night-loop, you don't need to download anything: open it and it updates itself.

## New

- **Seasonal themes follow your country.** Night-loop reads your Windows region setting. It doesn't use your location, and it saves and sends nothing.
  - **China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore, Malaysia, Korea and Vietnam:** Lunar New Year (red lanterns, fireworks, a pagoda and a red envelope) and the Mid-Autumn Festival (a full harvest moon with the jade rabbit, floating lanterns, mooncakes and tea).
  - **United States:** the 4th of July and Thanksgiving week in November.
  - **Canada:** Thanksgiving week in October.
  - St. Patrick's Day shows in the US, Ireland, the UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand.
