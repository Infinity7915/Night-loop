> **Still an early test build.** Please report anything odd in [Issues](https://github.com/Infinity7915/Night-loop/issues).

If you already have Night-loop, you don't need to download anything: open it and it updates itself.

## New

- **Seasonal themes follow your country.** Night-loop reads your Windows region setting. It doesn't use your location, and it saves and sends nothing.
  - **China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore, Malaysia, Korea and Vietnam:** Lunar New Year (red lanterns, fireworks, a pagoda and a red envelope) and the Mid-Autumn Festival (a full harvest moon with the jade rabbit, floating lanterns, mooncakes and tea).
  - **United States:** the 4th of July and Thanksgiving week in November.
  - **Canada:** Thanksgiving week in October.
  - St. Patrick's Day shows in the US, Ireland, the UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand.
  - **India and Nepal:** Diwali (diyas, rangoli and marigolds) and Holi (clouds of color)
  - **Diwali also in** Singapore, Malaysia, Sri Lanka, Fiji, Mauritius, Trinidad, Guyana and Suriname
  - **Muslim-majority countries:** Eid al-Fitr (lanterns, a crescent and star, a mosque)
  - **Mexico:** Day of the Dead (papel picado, a sugar skull, marigolds and candles)
  - **Brazil:** Carnival (a feathered mask and confetti)
  - **Japan:** Hanami (cherry blossoms, a torii gate and dango)
  - **Canada:** Canada Day (fireworks and the maple leaf flag)
  - **France:** Bastille Day (the Eiffel Tower and fireworks)
  - **UK:** Bonfire Night (a bonfire, sparklers and a toffee apple)
  - **Australia:** Australia Day (the Southern Cross, a kangaroo and a surfboard)
  - **Netherlands:** King's Day (a windmill, tulips and orange bunting)
  - **Germany and Austria:** Oktoberfest (a pretzel, a stein and blue-white bunting)
- Country holidays only show in that country, at the right time of year. Halloween shows in the US, Canada, the UK, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand.
- **Global themes** switch: shows every country's holidays when it's their time, not only your region's. Only one of Seasonal themes and Global themes can be on at a time. When two holidays overlap, the one whose main day is closer wins. If their main days are within 3 days of each other, it's a 50/50 pick for the day.
