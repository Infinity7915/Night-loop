> **Still an early test build.** Please report anything odd in [Issues](https://github.com/Infinity7915/Night-loop/issues).

If you already have Night-loop, you don't need to download anything: open it and it updates itself.

## Fixed

- **A macro could stop with no message.** When a macro started with its key from Saved macros hit an error, it
  just ended, so a Repeat until or Go to looked broken. Every error now shows in the status bar and as a Windows
  notification, with the block number and what to fix.
- **"Image visible" without a picture** now says "no picture yet" on the If / Repeat until row. Such a check can't
  run: press **Capture image from screen** on that block to give it its picture.

## Download

- **NightLoop-win-Setup.exe**: installs Night-loop for your Windows user and keeps it up to date.
- **NightLoop-win-Portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `NightLoop.exe`.

Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"** because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info →
Run anyway**.
