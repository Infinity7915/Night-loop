## Night-loop 0.10.3

- **Wait blocks wait until it's found.** New Wait for text, Wait for image and Wait for pixel blocks (and their "gone" versions) start with the timeout set to Infinite. Before, they gave up after 10 seconds (5 for pixels) and went on, which looked like the wait didn't wait. Blocks you already made keep their timeout: clear the box to make one Infinite.
