> **Still an early test build.** Please report anything odd in [Issues](https://github.com/Infinity7915/Night-loop/issues).

If you already have Night-loop, you don't need to download anything: open it and it updates itself.

## Fixed

- **Scan & turn and Find & click kept going after they found and clicked their target.** The block itself
  stops after its click, but inside a Loop (or with the macro set to repeat) the loop ran it again straight
  away, so it turned and clicked over and over. Both blocks now have an **After found** choice:
  - **Go on to next block** (the default, same as before)
  - **Leave the loop**: jump past the End of the Loop or Repeat until it sits in. Outside a loop, the macro stops.
  - **Go to label**
  - **Stop macro**

  Pick **Stop macro** or **Leave the loop** to have it click once and be done.

## Download

- **NightLoop-win-Setup.exe**: installs Night-loop for your Windows user and keeps it up to date.
- **NightLoop-win-Portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `NightLoop.exe`.

Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"** because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info →
Run anyway**.
