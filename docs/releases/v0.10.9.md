## Night-loop 0.10.9

- **Dimmer or brighter is back, next to Brightness range %.** With Match colors on, "Dimmer or brighter" set to Average colors evens the colors out to the picture's brightness, so any dimmer or brighter copy matches. Set to Use range, Brightness range % says how much dimmer or brighter a copy may be (blank = 3, and a little screen noise is always allowed).
- **Brightness range works when the picture is found at another size.** A dimmer copy was often found a little too small, and the check then only saw its brighter middle, so copies 20% dimmer passed a 6% range. The brightness is now measured where the picture's colors line up best.
