# What's new in 0.6.2

- Match colors: picture blocks now check colors as well as shape, so a look-alike in another color is never clicked. On by default. Capture older pictures again to use it.
- Fixed: centering on a target (Scan & turn, Center on target) no longer tilts the camera up or down. It turns sideways only, and a click in games where the camera follows the mouse tilts back level afterwards.

# What's new in 0.6.1

- Pictures are found at any size automatically, from 30% to 160% of how you captured them, so far-away targets are still found and clicked. This works on the picture blocks you already have.

# What's new in 0.6.0

- Undo and redo in the block editor (Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y). Deleted blocks come back, up to 200 steps.
- Copy, cut and paste blocks (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+V). Pasted blocks go under the highlighted block.
- The Delete key removes the ticked blocks.
- Change these keys in Settings › Macros › Editor shortcuts. Macros can't use a shortcut key as their start key.
- Find & click and Scan & turn now have Confidence % and Color tolerance.
- Fixed: a macro's "Delete?" button stayed armed after a mis-click. It now resets when you click anything else or switch tabs.
