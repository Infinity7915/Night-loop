# What's new in 0.6.8

- Fixed: opening one macro after another no longer swaps their names in Saved macros.

# What's new in 0.6.7

- Fixed: with Pick on the live screen on, pictures no longer include the purple outline, so their colors match. Capture those pictures again.

# What's new in 0.6.6

- Fixed: the pinned bars at the top of a long macro no longer leave a thin gap above them while scrolling.

# What's new in 0.6.5

- New: Pick on the live screen (Settings › Macros, off by default). Picking a click spot, color, area or picture no longer freezes the screen. A crosshair, the position and the outline show where you're picking, and pictures are taken the moment you let go.
- New: with a long macro, the bars with Record, Play and Add block stay pinned to the top as you scroll, like the block editor.

# What's new in 0.6.4

- Fixed: Turn with Arrow keys now really presses the arrow keys, so Scan & turn turns the camera and stops getting stuck.
- Fixed: the right mouse button no longer stays held after Scan & turn finds its target.

# What's new in 0.6.3

- Center camera on it (Scan & turn) and Center on target now put the target right in the middle in two steps: first they turn sideways until it is centered left to right, then they look up or down at it. They never turn both ways at once.

# What's new in 0.6.2

- Match colors: picture blocks now check colors as well as shape, so a look-alike in another color is never clicked. On by default. Capture older pictures again to use it.
- Fixed: centering on a target (Scan & turn, Center on target) no longer tilts the camera up or down. It turns sideways only, and a click in games where the camera follows the mouse tilts back level afterwards.

# What's new in 0.6.1

- Pictures are found at any size automatically, from 30% to 160% of how you captured them, so far-away targets are still found and clicked. This works on the picture blocks you already have.

# What's new in 0.6.0

- Undo and redo in the block editor (Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y). Deleted blocks come back, up to 200 steps.
- Copy, cut and paste blocks (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+V). Pasted blocks go under the highlighted block.
- The Delete key removes the ticked blocks.
- Change these keys in Settings › Macros › Editor shortcuts. Macros can't use a shortcut key as their start key.
- Find & click and Scan & turn now have Confidence % and Color tolerance.
- Fixed: a macro's "Delete?" button stayed armed after a mis-click. It now resets when you click anything else or switch tabs.
